INTERIOR SHOT. The camera is inside the living room, one warm room pinned for every shot of this song.

A warm family living room in soft orange, teal and wood tones: a big rounded comfy
sofa with colourful cushions, a cosy armchair with a knitted blanket, a low wooden
coffee table, a soft round rug with a few wooden toys and a ball on it, framed
picture shapes on the wall, a tall potted plant. A large curtained window looks out
on running rain, a soft grey sky and a blurred green garden. Cool grey daylight from
the window, warm lamplight from inside.
