EXTERIOR NIGHT on the terrace. Children stay on the deck planks: never in the sky, on the railing, roof or indoors.

A honey-plank terrace deck beside a mint-green cartoon house; its arched glass door glows amber. A cream balustrade runs along the open edges; on the deck a round table, two chairs, a woven mat. Far below, a sleeping pastel village. Indigo sky, small white stars, lilac clouds, thin crescent moon. Cool blue moonlight, warm amber door light.
