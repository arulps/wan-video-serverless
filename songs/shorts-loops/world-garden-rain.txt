EXTERIOR DAY in the small back garden during a gentle monsoon rain; everyone stays inside the garden.

A small, safe back garden in the rain: soft wet green lawn, round bushes, a low wooden fence, a clump of tall colocasia plants with huge heart-shaped leaves and a young banana plant. Gentle rain falls in soft silver streaks; cool grey-green daylight with a soft glow, raindrops shining on the leaves.
