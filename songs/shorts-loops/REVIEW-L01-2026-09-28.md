## What this says
1. **Phantom cannot do "hidden → reveal".** It is conditioned to show the reference subject; asking for her to be hidden
   gives empty rooms, pop-ins or a floating head. Peekaboo-type loops (L01, L13 blanket lump, L17 door dash) are the
   wrong job for Phantom.
2. **Loops need first/last-frame control.** Phantom has no keyframe input. For loop Shorts the right tool is a
   first-and-last-frame model (Wan first-last-frame-to-video, ComfyUI `WanFirstLastFrameToVideo`): one still of the
   start pose used as both the first and last frame guarantees the loop; identity comes from the still. To verify:
   the model files, a distill LoRA for it, and cost.
3. **480 vs 720 not settled by this set.** The compositions differ too much (seed × size) and the 720p clips framed
   her tiny ("whole curtain + full body" in a tall frame). What it does show: at 720p→4K the image is clean and sharp;
   the character size is decided by the framing words, not the resolution.
4. **Upscale speed:** 720p sources took 7.5–9 s/frame vs 3.5–3.8 for 480p because the x4 pass makes 2880×5120 then
   shrinks. Fix: use x3 for 720p sources (720×3 = 2160 exactly), ≈ 2× faster. Small change in `upscale_video.py`.
