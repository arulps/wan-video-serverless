INTERIOR DAY in the family living room while it rains outside; everyone stays inside the room.

A warm family living room in soft orange, teal and wood tones: a big rounded sofa with colourful cushions, a cosy armchair with a knitted blanket, a soft round rug with a few wooden toys, a tall potted plant, and a large curtained window looking out on running rain, a soft grey sky and a blurred green garden. Cool grey daylight from the window, warm lamplight inside.
