EXTERIOR DAY in a sunny park.

A big, safe, sunny neighbourhood park on a bright, warm day: soft green lawns, a winding pale path, round bushes, a leafy green hedge, a few big shady trees, and colourful flower beds — a big red rose bed, a white jasmine bush with small star-shaped flowers, bright orange marigolds and tall sunflowers. Clear blue sky with a few puffy round clouds, warm even sunshine.
