EXTERIOR NIGHT, camera on the terrace. Every child stays on the deck planks: never in the sky, on the railing, on the roof, or indoors.

A warm honey-plank terrace deck beside a mint-green cartoon house; its arched glass
door glows amber. A cream balustrade of turned balusters runs along the open edges.
On the deck a round table, two chairs, a woven mat near the railing. Far below, a
sleeping pastel village, one or two windows lit. Indigo sky, small white stars,
lilac clouds, thin crescent moon. Cool blue moonlight, warm amber door light.
