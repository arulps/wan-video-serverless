EXTERIOR NIGHT SHOT. The camera is outside on the terrace. Every child is on the wooden terrace deck, standing, sitting or lying on the planks. The stars, the crescent moon and the sleeping village are far above and far beyond the terrace railing. Never place a child in the sky, on the railing, on the roof or off the deck, and never move the scene indoors.

A raised wooden terrace deck of warm honey-coloured planks, attached to a small
cartoon house with mint-green walls, a slate-blue tiled pitched roof, a tall
arched double glass door with wooden lattice panes glowing warm amber from
inside, a small round window above the door and an arched side window. A
cream-painted balustrade of turned balusters with round ball finials runs along
the two open edges of the deck. On the deck stand a small round wooden table
with two little wooden chairs, a terracotta pot holding a small round topiary
tree, a low potted bush, and a soft woven mat spread flat near the railing. Far
beyond and below the railing lie the small dark rounded shapes of a sleeping
pastel village, one or two windows still warm and lit, rounded fruit trees and
palms, and soft dark hills. Above everything a deep indigo night sky graduating
to soft violet near the horizon, scattered with many small bright white stars,
drifting soft lilac clouds, and a thin pale crescent moon. Cool blue moonlight
across the deck, warm amber light spilling out of the glass door.
