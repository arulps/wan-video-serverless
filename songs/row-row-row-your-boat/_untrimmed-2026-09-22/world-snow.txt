EXTERIOR SHOT ON THE RIVER. All three characters stay inside the wooden rowing boat, sitting on its seats, for the whole shot. The water, the banks and any animal are outside the boat, never in it. Never put a child in the water, never put a child on the bank, and never leave the boat empty.

A gentle storybook river between winter banks: wide calm soft blue water with small rounded discs of pale ice drifting on it, soft snow-dusted white banks with rounded ice ledges at the water edge, a few bare rounded trees with snow on their branches, a pale bright winter sky. Cool clear winter light, tiny snowflakes falling slowly, small puffs of breath in the air.

The boat is a friendly rounded small wooden rowing boat with two oars. Appa sits amidships facing the stern with both oars, Minnu sits in the bow, Mintu sits in the stern.
