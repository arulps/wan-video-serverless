SNOWY WINTER RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat, animals outside it; no child in the water or on the bank.

A storybook river between winter banks: calm blue water with drifting discs of pale ice, snow-dusted white banks, pale winter sky, cool clear light, tiny snowflakes falling slowly.
A small wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both oars, the girl in the bow, the boy in the stern.
