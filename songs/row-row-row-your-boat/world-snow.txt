SNOWY WINTER RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat; animals stay outside it. No child in the water or on the bank; boat never empty.

A gentle storybook river between winter banks: calm blue water with small rounded discs of pale ice drifting, snow-dusted white banks with rounded ice ledges at the water edge, pale winter sky, cool clear light, tiny snowflakes falling slowly, puffs of breath.
A small rounded wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both, Minnu in the bow, Mintu in the stern.
