GOLDEN GRASSLAND RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat; animals stay outside it. No child in the water or on the bank; boat never empty.

A gentle storybook river: calm blue water, banks of tall waving golden grass, warm flat rocks at the water edge, blue sky, puffy white clouds, bright sun, water sparkling.
A small rounded wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both, Minnu in the bow, Mintu in the stern.
