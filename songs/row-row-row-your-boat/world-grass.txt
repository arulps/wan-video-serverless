GOLDEN GRASSLAND RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat, animals outside it; no child in the water or on the bank.

A storybook river through golden grassland: calm blue water, tall golden grass on both banks, a few rounded rocks, wide bright sky, warm afternoon light.
A small wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both oars, the girl in the bow, the boy in the stern.
