SECRET REED CHANNEL: everyone stays seated in the boat, animals outside it; no child in the water or on the bank.

A narrow secret channel through tall green reeds on both sides, everything oversized around the tiny boat, dappled green light, calm dark-blue water, dragonflies.
A small wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both oars, the girl in the bow, the boy in the stern.
