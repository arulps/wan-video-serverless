SECRET REED CHANNEL: everyone stays seated in the boat; animals stay outside it. No child in the water or on the bank; boat never empty.

A narrow secret green channel off the river: soft blue water barely wider than the boat, huge round lily pads floating, tall green reeds arching overhead on both sides, dappled emerald light through the leaves; everything oversized.
A small rounded wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both, Minnu in the bow, Mintu in the stern.
