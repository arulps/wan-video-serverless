GOLDEN EVENING RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat; animals stay outside it. No child in the water or on the bank; boat never empty.

A gentle storybook river in warm golden late-afternoon light: calm water turning soft gold with long golden reflections, low green banks and rolling hills, a warm amber sky, low golden sun.
A small rounded wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both, Minnu in the bow, Mintu in the stern.
