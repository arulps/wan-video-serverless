GOLDEN EVENING RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat, animals outside it; no child in the water or on the bank.

A storybook river at golden evening: calm water glowing orange-gold, banks in warm shadow, a low amber sun, soft pink-gold sky, long gentle reflections.
A small wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both oars, the girl in the bow, the boy in the stern.
