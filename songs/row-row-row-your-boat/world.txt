ON THE RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat; animals stay outside it. No child in the water or on the bank; boat never empty.

A gentle storybook river in bright daylight: wide calm blue water, grassy green banks with rounded stones and soft reeds, round bushes, blue sky with puffy white clouds, water sparkling.
A small rounded wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both, Minnu in the bow, Mintu in the stern.
