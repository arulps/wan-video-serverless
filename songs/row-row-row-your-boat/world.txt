ON THE RIVER: everyone stays seated in the boat, animals outside it; no child in the water or on the bank.

A storybook river in bright daylight: calm blue water, grassy green banks with rounded stones and reeds, blue sky with puffy white clouds, water sparkling.
A small wooden rowing boat with two oars: Appa amidships facing the stern holds both oars, the girl in the bow, the boy in the stern.
